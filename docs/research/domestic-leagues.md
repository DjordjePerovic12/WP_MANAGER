# Domestic league and cup formats (research, October 2026)

Collected from public pages: Wikipedia, federation sites and news. Many 2026-27 pages aren't published yet, so 2025-26 is used where needed. Points marked ⚠ are inferred or uncertain.

## Across most leagues

- **No draws** in most leagues: Italy, Hungary, France and the European cups. A level game goes to penalties, scored **3 / 2 / 1 / 0** (win, shootout win, shootout loss, loss).
- **The champion comes from playoffs, not the regular season.** Finals are usually best-of-5 (Italy, Croatia, Greece, Romania, Serbia), sometimes best-of-3 (Spain, Malta).
- **Relegation:** play-outs or a relegation series against the second division.

## Regional League (formerly the Adriatic League): suspended

- **The old league:** the Regional League ran 2008–2025 for Serbia, Montenegro, Croatia, Bosnia and Slovenia, with divisions A1 and A2. It has been **frozen since 2025-26**.
- **VRL, its replacement since summer 2025:** Serbia and Montenegro only. It continues in 2026-27, and Croatian clubs play only their national league.
- **VRL Premier League 2026-27, 12 clubs:**
  - Serbia: Novi Beograd, Partizan, Zemun, Radnički, Šabac, Vojvodina, Nais, Valis.
  - Montenegro: Jadran Herceg Novi, Primorac Kotor, Budućnost, Budva.
- **VRL First League 2026-27, 9 clubs:**
  - Serbia: Beograd, Košutnjak, Novobeogradski Vukovi, Stari Grad, Sava, Spartak Subotica.
  - Montenegro: Cattaro, Mladost Bijela, Sutjeska.
- **Format:** probably a double round robin, then a **Final Four**. In 2025-26 the Final Four was in Kragujevac in April: Radnički beat Primorac 17–11 in the final.
- **Serbia's national title** is decided after the VRL, by a knockout seeded by VRL position. The semi-finals are best-of-3, the final best-of-5 ⚠. It runs from late April into May and June. The **Serbian Cup** has a Final Four in December.
- **Montenegro** has a separate small national league ⚠ and a cup.
- **Croatia, Prva HVL, 11 clubs:** Jadran Split, Jug, Mladost, Medveščak, Mornar, Solaris, POŠK, Primorje, Zadar 1952, OVK Split, KPK Korčula.
  - Format: a league, then a top-8 playoff ⚠ with a best-of-5 final. Mladost were the 2026 champions.
  - **Croatian Cup:** Final Four in December.
  - Two league rounds a week in October.

## Malta (ASA, BOV leagues)

- **Divisions:**
  - Premier: Neptunes, San Giljan, Sliema, Sirens, Exiles, Valletta United.
  - First: Birżebbuġa, Marsaxlokk, Marsaskala, Ta' Xbiex, Otters (Gozo), Barracudas.
- **The main season is the summer:**
  - A double round robin of 10 rounds, late June to late July.
  - Playoffs in August: semi-finals 1v4 and 2v3, then a best-of-3 final ⚠. Neptunes were 2026 champions ⚠.
- **Summer Knock-Out:** a 6-club cup played between the league and the playoffs. The top 2 get byes. San Giljan won in 2026.
- **Off-season competitions:** a Winter League, the President's Cup and the Enemed Cup.
- **Many foreign players join for the summer.**
- **Europe:** Maltese clubs play in the Challenger Cup.

## Hungary, OB I

- **Clubs:** 14 in 2025-26 (FTC, Honvéd, BVSC, Vasas, Szolnok, Szeged, OSC, Eger, Debrecen, Szentes, UVSE, Kaposvár, KSI, Miskolc). The 2026-27 list is ⚠.
- **Format, 2025-26:**
  1. A single round robin with no draws.
  2. The top 8 go to an Upper House, the bottom 6 to a Lower House that also includes the top 2 of OB I/B.
  3. Semi-finals scored with a points system; series decide places 5–8.
- **The format changes almost every season** (2024-25 had best-of-3 quarter-finals).
- **2026 final standings:** FTC, Honvéd, BVSC, Szolnok, Vasas.
- **Cups:** Magyar Kupa and a Super Cup ⚠.

## Italy, Serie A1

- **14 clubs:** Pro Recco, Brescia, Savona, Posillipo, Canottieri Napoli, Ortigia, Telimar Palermo, De Akker Bologna, Trieste, Quinto, Olympic Roma, Roma Vis Nova, Lazio and Chiavari.
- **Regular season:** a double round robin of 26 rounds from 3 October, scored 3/2/1.
- **After the regular season:**

  | Places | What they play |
  |---|---|
  | 1–4 | Title playoffs (semi-finals best-of-3 ⚠, final best-of-5) |
  | 5–8 | Conference Cup playoff |
  | 10–13 | Play-out |
  | 14 | Relegated directly |

- **The 2026 final:** Recco beat Brescia 3–0.
- **Serie A2:** North and South groups. Plus the Coppa Italia (Final Four or Final Eight in spring) and the Supercoppa.

## Spain, División de Honor (official RFEN 2026-27 rules)

- **12 clubs:** Barceloneta, Sabadell, CN Barcelona, Terrassa, Mataró, Sant Andreu, Tenerife Echeyde, Rubí, Ceuta, Mediterrani, Catalunya, Canoe.
- **Regular season:** a double round robin of 22 rounds.
- **Title playoff:** two-legged semi-finals 1v4 and 2v3, decided by wins, then goal difference. The final is best-of-3.
- **European playoff:** two legs, 5v8 and 6v7.
- **Relegation:** two-legged semi-finals 9v12 and 10v11. The two losers play a best-of-3, and the loser is relegated.
- **Copa del Rey:** a Final Eight in March for the top 8 at the halfway point.
- **Supercopa** at the start of the season.

## Greece, A1 Ethniki

- **14 clubs:** Olympiacos, Vouliagmeni, Panathinaikos, Ethnikos, Hydraikos, Peristeri, Apollon Smyrnis, Panionios, Chios, PAOK, Palaio Faliro, Chania, Glyfada, Ilysiakos.
- **Regular season:** a double round robin of 26 rounds from 26 September.
- **After the regular season:**

  | Places | What they play |
  |---|---|
  | 1–4 | Playoffs: semi-finals best-of-3, final best-of-5 |
  | 5–8 | European playoff |
  | 11–14 | Play-out, with 2 relegated |

- **Olympiacos** have been champions every season since 2013. There is also a cup and a super cup ⚠.

## Germany, DWL (1. Bundesliga)

- **8 clubs:** Waspo Hannover, Spandau 04, ASC Duisburg, OSC Potsdam, White Sharks Hannover, SG Neukölln, SV Cannstatt, SV Krefeld 72.
- **Regular season:** a double round robin of 14 rounds, October to March.
- **Playoffs:** all 8 clubs play best-of-3 quarter-finals, then semi-finals and a best-of-5 final ⚠. Places 5–8 are decided by series.
- **Relegation:** the last club plays a best-of-3 against the 2. Bundesliga runner-up.
- **Cups:** the DSV-Pokal, a knockout from the autumn, and a Supercup in late September.

## France, Élite

- **10 clubs:** Marseille, Strasbourg, Pays d'Aix, Nice, Sète, Montpellier, Taverny, Douaisis, Bordeaux, Monaco.
- **Regular season:** a double round robin of 18 rounds, scored 3/2/1.
- **Playoffs, top 6:**
  - 1 and 2 get byes; the quarter-finals are 3v6 and 4v5.
  - Quarter-finals and semi-finals are two legs, with penalties if level on aggregate.
  - The final is two legs, plus a decider if level.
- **Relegation:** the last club plays a two-legged barrage against the Nationale 1 runner-up.
- **Cup:** the Trophée Pierre Garsau, a mid-season tournament for the top 8.
