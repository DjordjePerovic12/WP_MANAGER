# European Aquatics men's club competitions (research, October 2026)

This is collected from public pages: Wikipedia season articles and news on europeanaquatics.org. It hasn't been checked against the official regulations, which I couldn't find. Points marked ⚠ are inferred or uncertain.

The format has been in use since 2023-24, and 2026-27 keeps it.

## Rules shared by all competitions

- **How places are decided.** Each country's places come from an association ranking built on the last 4 seasons of club results. The 2026-27 order:

  | Rank | Country | Points |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | ITA | 97,527.5 |
  | 2 | HUN | 81,337.5 |
  | 3 | ESP | 77,410 |
  | 4 | SRB | 69,680 |
  | 5 | GRE | 66,635 |
  | 6 | CRO | 63,177.5 |
  | 7 | FRA | 36,745 |
  | 8 | ROU | 26,700 |
  | 9 | GER | 25,400 |
  | 10 | MNE | 24,565 |
  | 11 | GEO | 9,727.5 |
  | 12 | TUR | 8,110 |

- **No replacements:** if a club declines its place, the place goes unused.
- **Group points:** 3 for a win in regular time, 2 for a shootout win, 1 for a shootout loss, 0 for a loss. A group game level after regular time goes straight to penalties, so group games never end as draws.
- **Group tiebreakers, in order:** head-to-head points, head-to-head goal difference, head-to-head goals scored, overall goal difference, overall goals scored.
- **Title holders qualify for:**

  | Winner of | Plays next season in |
  |---|---|
  | Champions League | Champions League |
  | Euro Cup | Champions League |
  | Conference Cup | Euro Cup |
  | Challenger Cup | Conference Cup |

  The place goes unused if the club qualifies through its league anyway. There are no wildcards.
- **Draws** are held in Zagreb, seeded by club ranking.

## Champions League

**Who enters:** the 1st and 2nd-placed clubs from associations ranked 1–10, and the champions of ranks 11–12. In 2026-27 that's 21 clubs: 10 go straight to the group stage and 11 play qualification.

**Qualification**
- Mid-September, Friday to Sunday.
- Three round-robin tournaments at single venues, with groups of 4, 4 and 3.
- The top 2 of each go to the group stage, which makes 6 places.
- Clubs finishing 3rd or 4th go straight into the Euro Cup group stage.

**Group stage**
- 16 clubs in 4 groups of 4, playing home and away: 6 matchdays from October to mid-December.
- Played on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.
- The top 2 go to the quarter-final round.
- Clubs finishing 3rd or 4th drop into the Euro Cup eighth-finals.

**Quarter-final round**
- Not a knockout: 8 clubs play in 2 groups of 4, home and away, 6 matchdays from February to April.
- Clubs that met in the group stage are kept apart.
- The top 2 of each group go to the Final Four.

**Final Four**
- Single games: semi-finals, a 3rd-place game and the final.
- 2026-27: 13–15 May 2027 in Belgrade.

**2026-27 groups:**

| Group | Clubs |
|---|---|
| A | Olympiacos, Waspo'98 Hannover, Jadran Herceg Novi, Sabadell |
| B | Pro Recco, Radnički Kragujevac, Brescia, Oradea |
| C | Barceloneta (holders), Mladost Zagreb, Novi Beograd, Jadran Split |
| D | FTC, Marseille, Panathinaikos, Steaua |

**Prize money:** unknown. One report mentions €125,000 for the winner. ⚠

## Euro Cup

**Who enters:** the 3rd and 4th-placed clubs from ranks 1–10, and the 1st and 2nd from ranks 11–12.

**Qualification**
- 2026-27: early October, 22 clubs in 5 round-robin tournaments at single venues, producing 11 places.
- Clubs that fail to qualify go to the Conference Cup.

**Group stage**
- 16 clubs in 4 groups of 4, home and away, played on Thursdays.
- The 16 are the 11 qualifiers plus the 5 clubs that finished 3rd–4th in Champions League qualification. ⚠
- The top 2 go to the eighth-finals.

**Knockout rounds**
- The 8 Euro Cup clubs from the groups, plus the 8 clubs that finished 3rd–4th in Champions League groups.
- Champions League 4th-placed clubs are drawn against Euro Cup group winners, and 3rd-placed clubs against runners-up.
- Every round, the final included, is played over **two legs** and decided on aggregate: eighth-finals, quarter-finals, semi-finals, final.
- First legs are usually on Wednesday, second legs on Saturday.
- Level on aggregate: probably a penalty shootout straight after the second leg. ⚠

## Conference Cup (third tier, new in 2025-26)

**Who enters:** the 5th and 6th-placed clubs from ranks 1–10, the 3rd–5th from ranks 11–12, and the champions of rank 13 and below.

**Format** (2025-26):

| Round | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Qualification round I | November | Groups at single venues |
| Qualification round II | February | The qualifiers from round I plus 8 clubs that failed Euro Cup qualification |
| Quarter-final round | March | 2 groups of 4 at single venues |
| Final Four | May | Single games |

The top 2 go through at every stage.

## Challenger Cup (fourth tier)

**Who enters:** associations ranked 13 and below, up to 6 clubs each, plus clubs dropping down from the Conference Cup.

**Format** (2025-26):
- Qualification round I in late November and round II in February, both as groups.
- **Final Eight** in March: 2 groups of 4, then single-game semi-finals and a final.
- Knockout games level after regular time go straight to penalties.

Malta hosts qualification groups: San Giljan hosts in 2026-27.

## Super Cup

One match at the end of September between the Champions League winner and the Euro Cup winner.
